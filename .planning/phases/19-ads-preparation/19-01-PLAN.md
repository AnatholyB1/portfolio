| T-19-01 | Tampering | assessUtm / canonicaliseUtmValue | mitigate | closed vocabularies and anchored regexes; only source/medium aliases are rewritten; campaign/content are flagged, never rewritten; input already capped at 200 chars by parseAttrParams |
| T-19-02 | Denial of service | CAMPAIGN_RE / CONTENT_RE | mitigate | linear regexes without nested overlapping quantifiers; length checked before regex |
